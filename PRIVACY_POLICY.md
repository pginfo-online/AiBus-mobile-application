# Privacy Policy for AiBus

**Effective Date:** October 7, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 7, 2026  

Welcome to **AiBus** ("we", "our", or "us"), provided by **Worknai** (package name: `com.worknai.aibus`). We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. 

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application (**AiBus**) and our associated bus ticket booking services.

By accessing or using the AiBus application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not access or use the application.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide, manage, and facilitate bus ticket bookings and customer support.

### A. Personal Information You Provide to Us
When you book a bus ticket, contact customer support, or interact with our local shop network, you may provide:
* **Contact Information:** Full name, phone number, and email address.
* **Travel Details:** Boarding point, destination city, travel date, seat preferences, passenger names, age, and gender (required by bus operators and transport authorities for passenger manifests).
* **Communication Records:** Inquiries, feedback, or support requests submitted via phone (`8010097706`), messaging, or partner stores.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
* **Device & Usage Information:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, app crash logs, and network performance data to ensure app reliability and security.
* **Approximate Location:** General network-derived location to display relevant nearby booking shops and bus origin cities. We do not track continuous background GPS location.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
1. **To Process Bus Ticket Bookings:** Confirming reservations with bus operators (e.g., Online Go, Vighnaharta, Siya Ram, Keshari, Pancham, and other partners).
2. **To Send Booking Updates:** Delivering ticket confirmation, bus schedule changes, boarding point details, and travel alerts via SMS, phone call, or email.
3. **To Provide Customer Support:** Responding to inquiries, cancellations, refund requests, or complaints through our customer care helpline (`8010097706`).
4. **To Support Local Booking Network:** Enabling local partner shops to assist customers with ticket issuance and verification.
5. **Security & Fraud Prevention:** Detecting, preventing, and addressing fraudulent activities or technical issues.
6. **Legal & Regulatory Compliance:** Complying with transport authority rules, tax obligations, and statutory requirements across India.

---

## 3. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We **do not sell, rent, or trade** your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

1. **Bus Operators:** We share passenger details (name, contact number, boarding/drop points, seat numbers) with the relevant bus operators to issue tickets and facilitate your journey.
2. **Local Partner Shops / Agents:** Authorized local booking stores assisting you with booking verification and customer assistance.
3. **Service Providers:** Trusted third-party vendors who provide cloud hosting, SMS delivery, analytics, and error tracking services under strict confidentiality agreements.
4. **Legal Requirements:** If required by law, court order, governmental authorities, or law enforcement agencies to comply with legal processes or protect the rights, property, and safety of AiBus, our users, or the public.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical, administrative, and physical security measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 5. Data Retention and Deletion

* **Retention:** We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, complete travel bookings, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable tax and accounting laws.
* **Data Deletion Rights:** You have the right to request the deletion of your personal account and associated data. You may request data deletion at any time by contacting us at **support@worknai.com** or via our helpline at **8010097706**. Upon verification, we will securely delete or anonymize your personal information, subject to mandatory statutory retention requirements.

## 6. Children's Privacy

AiBus does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13. Bookings for minors must be made by a parent or legal guardian. If we learn that personal data of a child under 13 was collected without verified parental consent, we will promptly delete that data.
